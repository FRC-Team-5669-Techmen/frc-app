# Techmen scouting: event-day field guide

Use this with the team's approved build and event. The review branch has not yet been released to production; `RELEASE.md` and `FIELD_ACCEPTANCE.csv` track that decision. Practice in a training event first.

## Before leaving reliable Wi-Fi

1. Sign in with your own Techmen account on the phone/browser you will use. Open Scouting and the correct event. Wait for the event and your assigned robot/match to appear.
2. Press **Refresh**, then **Sync now**. Resolve any failed or conflicting saves. “All saved work synced” concerns local submissions; it does not mean Discord delivered a message or the venue has no newer data.
3. In **Teams → Pit map and directory**, confirm the Nexus event key with your lead and download the map. Check its source/time and a known pit against the venue. Each phone needs its own download. No map is better than a confidently wrong map.
4. Turn off connectivity, reopen the prepared event, and verify your assignments and map are usable. Reconnect afterward. First-time offline login, an unprepared event and uncached external video are unavailable.
5. Charge the phone and carry team-approved power. Keep the same browser/profile. Clearing site data, private-session closure or device storage eviction can remove local work; export a backup before any reset.

## Scout one robot

Open **Scout this**, check the match and robot number, then press **Scout match**. Follow that robot. A visible success is **Yes**, a visible unsuccessful attempt is **No**, and an obstructed view is **Did not see**. Use **Not applicable** only when the criterion does not apply. Leave unchecked work unrecorded. Never fill an unknown with a zero or infer a robot's scoring from its alliance total.

Add a short note with the phase/action and useful uncertainty. Press **Save observation** and wait for the form to close. **Saved on this device** means the browser committed it locally. **Synced** means the server confirmed the record. Discord has its own status. While submission is pending, the form prevents edits and repeated submission.

For a correction, open the existing observation. Saving creates a new version and retains the earlier evidence. If another version won first, use **Review conflict** to compare both proposals after refreshing; choose deliberately. Do not submit an unrelated “new” observation to hide a conflict.

## Review evidence and brief the drive team

Use **Teams → Review match evidence** to open a robot's full observation history. Search by match, scout or notes; **Next observations** reaches older entries. Choose **Disputed current matches** to inspect contradictory accounts together, then ask each author to review the footage or notes. Prior-event footage is a separate filter. A pending correction keeps the previous confirmed rate and master-list vote until synchronization; locally edited pit claims carry an explicit pending label.

In **Teams → Drive-team match brief**, enter the match and all six teams after checking the field schedule. The app does not infer alliance colors from schedule order. Agree on autonomous routes, roles, endgame coordination and contingencies with your partners. Inspect thin/disputed evidence and stale estimates; unknown capabilities are not failures. Wait for **Brief saved on this device**, then **Confirm lineup and review brief**. Export JSON for attributed records/versions and printable HTML for the drive team; open the HTML and use the browser's Print command. Changing the plan or evidence requires another review. A reopened brief restores the draft but requires fresh review. Keep both exports with your event backup: these plans are local to this account/device and are not automatically shared or sent to Discord.

## Work in the pits

Use **Teams → Pit report** for what a team claims. Match observations verify those claims separately. Check location source/time and visible contradictions rather than silently overwriting the imported address.

Use **Pit messages** for a specific team/robot question. **Reply to pit conversation** keeps the original author and your answer separate. Resolve the question when the answer is useful. Request Discord delivery only when your lead has approved and configured it. If the receipt is uncertain, check the destination before further action; a saved question is still saved even when delivery fails.

## Recover from an interruption

| What you see | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Saving draft… | Keep the page open until the latest draft reports saved. Export only after that acknowledgment. |
| Draft could not be read | Use the named **Retry loading…** button. Editing pauses to protect the unread draft. |
| Local / waiting to sync | Keep this browser's data. Reconnect and press **Sync now**. |
| Retry save | Retry the existing save; its original ID protects against duplicate logical submissions. |
| Conflict | Refresh, inspect both versions, then keep the server version or apply your reviewed proposal. |
| Recovered · awaiting review | Inspect the original request before **Retry original save**. Merely reconnecting never releases it. |
| Storage could not save | Keep the page open. The app has not promised durability for that change. Restore available space/browser storage and retry; involve your lead before clearing data. |

At the event footer, **Export event data** preserves synchronized evidence, pending saves, drafts, practice and the downloaded map in an account/event JSON archive. Keep it within the authorized team. Check that the download exists before resetting a device.

On a replacement browser, sign in as the same member and open the same event. Choose **Recover pending saves from a backup**, inspect the file, select recoverable saves and restore them. They are held locally. In **Saved work on this device**, review and retry each original request. Already present saves are skipped; conflicting local work is protected. The server checks prior receipts, current permissions and versions again.

This recovery control restores queued submissions only. Practice, unsubmitted drafts and maps remain in the archive for review with your lead. It never imports login state, manager permissions or a file's claim of successful synchronization. Do not rename IDs or copy records into the database. See `RECOVERY.md` for exact behavior.

## Strategy and shift leads

- Review **Event coverage** for missing pit reports, thin/disputed observations and prior-event context. Coverage is not strength. A match with unknown answers may add no capability trial.
- In **Manage event**, preview the imported roster and source results before saving. Preview the assignment rotation, gaps, breaks and workload before assigning it. Two leads should coordinate; a preview is not a server reservation.
- Keep an independent pick list. Inspect the master list's support, medians and DNP votes. Unranked teams contribute no vote; there is no hidden outlier deletion or invented Elo calculation.
- Compare three saved teams with the evidence beside them. Preview the proposed positions; cancel does not save. Apply only when the reasoning is sound and the server version is current.
- Use **Practice** for the eight-mission activity plan and frozen/reviewed footage workbook. Completion is not proficiency or certification. Mentor assessment and official IDEA Classroom records remain separate.
