## Built option: participation folding (0050)

On Team standing, Settings, **Automatic participation folding** is the one admin switch. The dry-run preview evaluates through the existing standing rules and mass-change hold, then rolls the evaluation back, including notices and mail. It lists who would fold today and each reason. A held run folds nobody. Only participation failures generated by the hours/meetings evaluation qualify, with their failed probation record; grades, discipline and onboarding-deadline proposals do not qualify merely because they propose removal.

When enabled, the daily check marks candidates `folded`. The roster separates them with the failure reason and fold date. A mentor or admin can keep an active member active until that override is withdrawn, or restore a folded member in one tap. Restoration dismisses the current proposal and leaves history intact. Accounts, roles and approval stay intact. Feature access predicates explicitly admit folded accounts, and folded driver documents are not flagged for deletion. Decision 07 still governs offboarding separately. Turning the setting off prevents future automatic folds; it does not undo an existing fold. Review the preview before choosing ON. No live setting change is part of this job.
