# 42 Should the event family hub ask drivers for everything handbook 12.3.2 asks, and should "driver paperwork required" be on for Beach Blitz?
- Raised: 2026-10-04 by the event family hub session (ledger 0003)
- Status: open
- Default if nobody decides: **what the prompt specified and the tree now
  does.** A driver ticks two checks, "I am 25 or older" and "I have a valid
  California license and insurance", and Beach Blitz is seeded with "Driver
  paperwork required" OFF, so a family's car takes riders as soon as it is
  offered. Mr. Pina was confirming school policy when this was written.
- Decided: --

## What is actually true right now

- The Archdiocese of Los Angeles Administrative Handbook, topic 12.3.2
  ("Transportation for Parishes and Schools", read 2026-10-04 at
  handbook.la-archdiocese.org), asks four things of a parent or volunteer who
  drives minors: at least 25 years old; a clean driving record for the past
  three years; a valid California Class C license; current California
  insurance. It also says "the location must keep on file a copy of that
  driver license and proof of current insurance".
- The hub asks the first, third and fourth as two ticks. It does NOT ask about
  the three-year driving record.
- The copy-on-file rule is the per-event switch "Driver paperwork required"
  (0005). ON: every family car shows Pending and takes no riders until a mentor
  marks that family's paperwork on file on the mentor page. OFF (the Beach
  Blitz seed): cars take riders at once.
- The same topic's single-minor rule ("may not be alone in a vehicle with a
  single minor who is not their own child") is the hub's one-minor rule, ON by
  default, read exactly that way: a car whose driver's own student is aboard
  may carry one other student.

## The options

**A. Today's default.** Two ticks, switch off. Fastest for families; the
copy-on-file rule is not enforced by the app.

**B. Add a third tick for the driving record, keep the switch off.** One more
tap per driver; still nothing on file.

**C. Add the third tick and turn the switch on for Beach Blitz.** Matches the
handbook as written. Costs a mentor collecting a license and insurance copy
from every driving family before Oct 30 and toggling each one on the mentor
page's Paperwork line; until then those cars show Pending.

Switching the paperwork rule is one tap on the mentor page (Setup). Adding the
third tick is a small migration (a column and a line in the progress rule)
plus one control on the Getting there step.
