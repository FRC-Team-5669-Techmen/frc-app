# 53 A pause button for hours?
- Raised: 2026-10-28 from feedback 72e5d473 (Azad: "pause button for the hours, so if someone needs to do something non frc related, they can pause their hours, and unpause it after their finished.")
- Status: open
- Default if nobody decides: **not built.** A member doing something that is not FRC signs out (with the mentor's approval, as every sign-out is) and signs back in after. Breaks inside a session stay inside it, as Mr. Pina set on 2026-10-17.
- Decided: --

## Why it is a question

Mr. Pina's sign-out rule (2026-10-17): a student signs in once for an extended session, breaks stay inside it, and leaving needs his approval with a note. A self-serve pause would let time stop and start without that approval, which is the thing the rule exists to prevent. On the other side, a long non-FRC stretch inside a session (homework, another club) is counted today unless someone signs out.

## Options

1. **No pause (default).** Sign out and back in; a mentor approves the sign-out.
2. **Pause with a reason, no approval:** the session keeps running but paused minutes are subtracted; the pause shows on the presence board and in the hours history so a mentor can see it.
3. **Pause that a mentor approves**, the same way a sign-out is approved.
