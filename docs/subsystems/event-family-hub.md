# Event family hub

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` (The subsystems) on 2026-10-19, so `CLAUDE.md` stays short. `CLAUDE.md` links here; keep this file current in the same commit as any change to what it describes.

- **Event family hub** (`supabase/migrations/0005_event_family_hub.sql` + its `_rls_test.sql`, `0006_beach_blitz_seed.sql`, `supabase/functions/event-family/`, `src/eventHub.js`, `src/csv.js`, `src/EventFamilyPage.jsx`, `src/EventHubBoards.jsx`, `src/TripsPage.jsx`, `src/TripsAdmin.jsx`, `src/EventHub.css`; ledger 0003, 2026-10-04). A per-event hub for offsite competitions, entered as data; Beach Blitz 2026 is the 0006 seed and nothing in `src/` names it. **Families have no account**: one invite per (event, student), whose emailed link `/e/<token>` is the only credential (128 random bits, only its SHA-256 stored, several live tokens per invite because each email mints a fresh one). The page is public and outside `ProtectedLayout` like `/parent/:token`, never touches a table, and calls the single-file `event-family` Edge Function (`verify_jwt = false`, service role), which forwards to ONE SQL entry point, `hub_family_call(token, action, args)`. **Every rule is in Postgres** (SECURITY DEFINER, pinned `search_path`): seat capacity under a `FOR UPDATE` lock on the car, the one-minor rule (handbook 12.3.2, read as "alone in a vehicle with a single minor who is not their own child", so a car with the driver's own student aboard may take one rider; a claim into the bad state is refused, a rider leaving into it turns the car red, emails mentors and blocks Left until a staff override with a reason), consent-gated visibility (driver phone only to that car's families with the driver's consent, rider phone only to their driver with their consent, pickup spot only to staff, pickup-taking drivers while open and the accepting driver after, allergy names staff-only), edit windows (a day's answers until its venue close, a seat or car until that car is Left, food until the meal starts, nothing after the event; the deadlines lock nothing), and phase completeness (`_hub_progress`). Students read `hub_member_board()` (the same sanitized board) and never write; staff read and write through `hub_staff_call()`, which runs the SAME rule functions, and edit setup tables directly under `is_staff()` RLS. Nobody, staff included, writes family data, seats or cars directly. Mail is an outbox the rules write and the function drains over the existing Gmail secrets, after each family action and on an hourly pg_cron tick (`private.event_hub_config`, its own row, null = paused); a daily pure-SQL job clears pickup spots and phone consents 14 days after an event ends. The family page autosaves every field ("Saved h:mm", "Not saved, retrying" with the input kept, unsaved values replayed from localStorage), keeps a family on the form until it presses Finish, and reuses the same `FamilyHub` component when a mentor edits a family. `/trips` and `/trips/<id>` are the member views (avatar menu, non-parents); `/trips/<id>/manage` is the mentor page (five readiness lines, families, staff boards, setup, CSV with decision 18's formula guard in `src/csv.js`, printable day sheets). Proven by the 0005 test (69 checks) on `tools/sql-harness/`, 30 mutants and a two-session seat race there, `tests/event-hub.test.js`, `tests/event-family-function.test.js`, and `npm run test:features` (`event-hub`, 100 checks). **Not live until** 0005 and 0006 are pasted, `event-family` is deployed with JWT verification off, and the `EVENT_HUB_CRON_SECRET` / config row are set (`docs/history/brave-noether-tyn6cb.md` has the steps); until then `/trips` says not set up yet and a family link cannot open. Decision 42 holds the driver-requirements question. **The open link** (`supabase/migrations/0007_event_hub_open_link.sql` + its test, `src/EventJoinPage.jsx`, `src/dev/fixture/features/eventhubjoin.js`; 2026-10-05, decision 43): `/join` and `/join/<event id>` are the Google-Form-style link a mentor sends to every parent and guardian (the mentor page shows it with Copy). The parent picks their student from those with this season's application, gives a name and email and ticks "I am this student's parent or guardian". If nobody has started for that student they go straight into the family page (`hub_join` creates the invite, mints a token, emails the link); once a family has started (a token exists, or answers were saved) the page is NOT opened and the link is emailed to the addresses already on that family, masked on screen, so picking a name never shows another family's answers. More than one parent: `hub_add_parent(token, email)` from the family page's "Add another parent or guardian" card. Both functions are called from the browser with the anon key (PostgREST /rpc), so 0007 needed no function redeploy; queued mail is sent by calling the deployed function's `resend_link` with a blank address, which sends the queue and nothing else. `/e` alone now redirects to `/join`, and a dead link points there; the old "Send me a link" form is gone. Mentors driving add cars on the mentor page's Carpool tab, which the open page links to. **The family page as rebuilt, and 0008** (`supabase/migrations/0008_event_hub_families.sql` + its test, `src/EventFamilyParts.jsx`, `src/EventHubControls.jsx`, `src/eventIcons.jsx`, `src/dev/fixture/features/eventhubfamilies.js`; 2026-10-05, decision 44): four parts (Who is coming, Rides, Food and health, Contacts and forms) and a Finish review, under a tracker reading "N of 4 parts done" with a tile per part naming what is left; an (i) card on every question; an answered option question shrinks to the chosen card, one the page assumed stays open and says so; seats are claimed right under the ride question (the whole-team board and the food board are folds); Finish lists each missing answer as a line that opens its part and flags a run with no seat; Event info is closed sections. 0008 lets a parent drive other students without their own (`drive_to`/`drive_home`; that car falls under the one-child rule), widens adults to 0 to 30, gives each email its own link so one parent can be removed (`hub_remove_guardian`, `hub_staff_remove_guardian`), lets a family take itself off the trip (`hub_remove_family`) and a mentor remove one (`hub_staff_remove_family`), lets mentors seat two students into an empty car that needs two (`hub_staff_place_pair`, the only way such a car fills: a family's first claim is refused, and the page no longer offers it), and makes the one-child rule permanent (`hub_events_one_minor_rule_on`). It also makes each coming day's way there and way home an answer the family gives (progress keys `ride_to`/`ride_home`): the Team carpool default still plans a seat but no longer counts as answered, and the page picks nothing until the parent does. Who is coming lists the days in date order (decision 45). Every page of the form carries an Event info / Parent GroupMe bar, Event info opens with the event website and the GroupMe, the carpool board and the food sign-up are feature cards, and a sign-up is deleted by the family ("Delete our sign-up", on Contacts and Finish) or by a mentor (a Delete button on each row of Families). All called through PostgREST, so no function redeploy; before 0008 is pasted the page offers none of it. **Households** (`supabase/migrations/0009_event_hub_households.sql` + its test, `src/dev/fixture/features/eventhubhousehold.js`; 2026-10-06): a parent with more than one student signs each up on the open link with the same email ("Add another student" on a family page fills the name, email and tick in). Families in one event sharing an email are siblings (`_hub_siblings`): the one-child rule treats a brother or sister as the driver's own child (`_hub_own_aboard`, `_hub_claim`), each page lists the others (`household` on every page `hub_family_call` returns) and opens them through `hub_household_open(token, invite)`, which mints a link only when the email on the link in use is on the other family; Contacts offers "Copy from <name>'s page" (fills only empty answers), Who is coming asks to count adults on one page only, and the family car is offered and labelled on the sibling's page. **No SQL comment may contain an apostrophe**: the Supabase SQL editor's statement splitter cut the 0008 test mid dollar-quote on one (`supabase/migrations/README.md`). **The roster and the names** (`supabase/migrations/0010_event_hub_roster_names.sql` + its test, `src/dev/fixture/features/eventhubroster.js`; 2026-10-06, ledger 0004): ONE rule, `_hub_roster_student`, decides who the hub lists (the open link, Add them, the readiness count): approved, active, the student role, neither mentor nor admin, and this season's application. A student who also holds LEAD is on it (before 0010 every copy excluded lead, so student leads could not be signed up). ONE name, `_hub_student_name`: the application's legal first and last name, the profile name only when there is no application, never anything containing an at sign or shaped like an email local part (`_hub_clean_name`); `_hub_family_surname` reads it too. A lead who is a hub family gets family scope on the family link (nothing on the family path reads the signed-in user) and still the staff board on `/trips`. The Setup captain picker is the one client-side naming site and only refuses an email. - **Family link recovery** (`supabase/migrations/0023_family_link_recovery.sql` + its test, `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0023.mjs`, `src/dev/fixture/features/familylink.js`, `tools/e2e/features/family-link.mjs`; 2026-10-22, ledger 0016). Found at Beach Blitz 2026: a parent mistyped her email at `/join`, so every link went to the wrong inbox, and nothing on the mentor page could send the right one. **`staff_send_trip_link(p_invite, p_email, p_student, p_trip)`** (staff only, SECURITY DEFINER, pinned `search_path`) queues one `resend` email per address on ONE family (or to one address, which must be on the family), each carrying a fresh link minted at send time by `hub_outbox_mint_link`; deduped per address per hour (`staff_link:<invite>:<email>:<UTC hour>`); refuses a family on an event that is over, a family with no email, and, by student, more than one coming trip without a title. It is a standalone function rather than a new `hub_staff_call` action, so 0005's `hub_staff_call` is not redefined and a re-paste of 0005 cannot drop it, and it is named outside the `hub_` prefix because the 0005 test pins the grants of every `hub_*` function. The mentor page's family detail opens on **Emails and links**: the email field and Save emails, Send link, Reset links (asks first, then revokes every live token), each answering on its own line, and Send link beside each address in the people list. The connector reaches it as the hand tool `send_family_link` (confirm). **The open link** keeps decision 43's masked addresses and adds "Not your email, or a typo? A mentor can fix it and resend the link." with a mailto to the event's first alert email (`trip_join_contact`, anon; no mailto when the event has none or before 0023). **The family link** (`/family/<token>`) shows a Trip sign-ups card from `family_hub_trips(token)` (that token's student only, events not over: title, first day, sign-up done or not, lock-in when open; no address, token or invite id) with "Send me the sign-up link" (`family_hub_send_link(token, event)`, to the addresses already on the sign-up, deduped per address per hour, answering a count). Before 0023 is pasted Send link says so, the mailto is absent and the card is not shown. Proven by the 0023 test (22 checks), 6 mutants, the connector proof and `test:features` (`family-link`, 44 checks).
