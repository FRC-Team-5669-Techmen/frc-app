# Human player leaderboard

Built by ledger 0025 (migration 0033) so choosing a human player rests on a record that predicts who will score the most FUEL at a real competition. Feed is treated as unlimited (pre-staged bins of FUEL), so the drills measure the thrower, not the feeder. The record is kept across seasons; the page scores one season at a time.

- **Page `/human-player`** (`src/HumanPlayerPage.jsx`, `src/HumanPlayer.css` on top of `src/BatteriesPage.css` and `src/MatchSim.css`; tokens only, so the light theme restyles it). Reached from the Human player tile on `/robot`, next to Drivers. A season picker (current season by default, every season, All time), a tally, the leaderboard (rank, name, score, match, rate, accuracy; your row marked), a "Not ranked yet" list that says what each player still needs, a "How the Human Player Score works" disclosure printed from `HP_RULES`, Log an attempt, and My attempts. Tapping a player opens their sheet: drill values, personal bests, share of the team best, attempts. Staff also get a player picker, Edit and Void on any attempt; a student voids their own attempt for 24 hours. Parent-only accounts are told it is for students and staff. Before 0033 is applied the page says Not set up yet.
- **Three drills.** Accuracy: a fixed set of shots (20 by default, set per attempt), made of shots. Rate: a timed burst (30 s by default, set per attempt), made and missed. Match run: the full match clock from `src/matchSim.js` (AUTO, the SCORING AUTO pause, TRANSITION SHIFT, SHIFTS 1 to 4 with the HUBS alternating, END GAME), never redefined here; FUEL made while our HUB was active, made while inactive (counts nothing) and missed.
- **The live run** (`LiveRun` in the page): full screen, for the person logging in the shop. Two big pads, +1 MADE and +1 MISS, with Count by 5 and Undo. Accuracy counts to the set size and stops. Rate counts the burst down. The match run draws the HUB order at random before the start and shows it ("BLUE HUB INACTIVE FIRST, our HUB is active in SHIFTS 1 and 3"), then calls the phases with the simulator sounds (`src/matchCues.js`, shared with `/match-sim`) and shows OUR HUB ACTIVE or INACTIVE; a make is filed active or inactive by the HUB state at the tap. When the run ends the counts drop into the form to check and save. Counts can also be typed by hand.
- **The score lives in ONE file, `src/humanPlayerScore.js`**, read by the page, the `/robot` tile and the connector's `list_human_players`. The SQL stores attempts and never scores. Per attempt: accuracy = made / shots, rate = made / seconds, match = FUEL made while active (a match run counts only when at least 90% of the clock ran). Per drill: the mean of the best 3 of the player's last 5 counted attempts (one lucky run cannot top the board). Ranked only with 3 counted attempts of every drill; otherwise "needs N more". Each drill is divided by the team best among players with 3 attempts of it (0 to 1). Score = round(100 x (0.5 x match + 0.3 x rate + 0.2 x accuracy)). Voided attempts count for nothing. These weights are proposed defaults.
- **Database (0033).** `hp_attempts` (member, date, drill, made, attempted, made_inactive, seconds, alliance, inactive_first, notes, logged_by, void columns; `made + made_inactive <= attempted`). Select for `_hp_member_ok()` (approved, active, student or staff, not parent-only). No client write grant. Writes are SECURITY DEFINER functions through `writeVia` with no direct fallback: `hp_log_attempt` (own, within 60 days; staff for any approved active student or staff member), `hp_edit_attempt` (staff; the drill stays), `hp_void_attempt` (staff; the player for 24 hours). Nothing is deleted. 0033 replaces `_merge_member_rules` with the four `hp_attempts` columns: apply it again after any re-paste of 0020, 0022, 0024, 0026, 0028, 0029 or 0031.
- **Connector.** `list_human_players` (read, `season: current | all`); the three writes are generated `call_app` entries that ask first.
- **Proof.** `0033_human_player_leaderboard_rls_test.sql` (15 checks) and `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0033.mjs` (12 mutants); `tests/human-player-score.test.js`, `tests/connector-human-player.test.js`; `tools/e2e/features/human-player.mjs` on fixture mode (`src/dev/fixture/features/humanplayer.js`), which drives the live match run with the clock advanced into SHIFT 1 and SHIFT 2.
