# Member standing

- **Member standing** (`supabase/migrations/0015_member_standing.sql` + its test, `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0015.mjs`, `supabase/functions/notice-mail/`, `src/standing.js`, `src/StandingPage.jsx`, `src/StandingTeamPage.jsx`, `src/Standing.css`, `src/dev/fixture/features/standing.js`, `tools/e2e/features/standing.mjs`, `tests/standing.test.js`; 2026-10-19, ledger 0010). Handbook Sections 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12 and 14. **Every rule is in SQL**; the pages read `member_standing(member)` and `staff_standing_overview()` and offer only what the RPCs accept.
  - **Absences**: `request_absence(event, reason, note, emergency)` for a team meeting or a mandatory event (a competition counts as mandatory): 24 hours ahead; an emergency (illness, family emergency, medical) until 11:59 PM the next weekday after the event; a mandatory event only by an emergency or a religious holiday 14 days ahead. `decide_absence` is staff, never for yourself. A request still pending when the event starts counts as excused (`_absence_excused`). The reasons the handbook never excuses are not offered.
  - **Weeks**: `team_weeks` break or work, posted through `staff_set_week` at least 14 days ahead; a break week never counts.
  - **The evaluation**: `standing_evaluate()`, daily by pg_cron at 13:10 UTC, or by an admin. It judges each finished Monday-to-Sunday week (`standing_weeks`: signed-in hours by `_member_hours`, the same pairing and 12-hour cap as `src/hoursUtils.js`, FLL and reviewed sessions excluded; 2 hours, 10 in build season from `standing_settings`) and each finished calendar month (`standing_months`: meetings attended minus every third late, against meetings that counted, never rounded; excused, break-week and suspension meetings do not count). A short week opens hours probation, a month under 80% opens meetings probation (`standing_records`), 4 clean weeks close it (an excused week extends it), and a miss or short week on probation fails it and PROPOSES removal. **A removal takes effect only when an admin confirms it** (`admin_confirm_standing`: the profile goes inactive and the family is emailed); an admin also confirms or withdraws each probation. Only members who are approved, active, students and not mentors or admins are judged, and nothing before `standing_settings.starts_on` (the Monday after 0015 was pasted) or before a member joined.
  - **Grades**: `admin_record_grade_check(member, date, below)`: no grades stored, only under 2.0 or not; under opens grades probation, at or above closes it, still under starts a new one.
  - **Records**: `discipline_records` through `staff_log_discipline` (verbal, written, suspension 1 to 4 weeks by a CHECK, removal admin only and immediate), appeals of a written warning within 7 days (`appeal_discipline`, `admin_decide_appeal`); `safety_violations` through `staff_log_safety` (one tap on `/standing/team` Log; the third in a school year, August to July, notifies every admin); `incident_reports` through `report_incident` (any member, from `/standing`) and `staff_update_incident` (parent called, front office told, closed).
  - **Voids**: `staff_void_session(member, reason)` from the Void button on `/signouts` ends an idle session and marks it voided in `session_reviews`; `staff_void_day(member, day, reason)` voids every session of a day.
  - **FLL mentors**: `fll_teams`, `fll_mentors`, `fll_join` (4 per team at most, under a row lock), `fll_leave`; the evaluation takes a mentor off a team after two weeks with no volunteer session.
  - **Notices**: `member_notices` in the app (read by the member, a linked parent and staff), and `notice_outbox` mail to the student and the parent emails on the newest application. The **`notice-mail`** Edge Function drains it every 10 minutes (`private.notice_mail_config`, null = paused; `NOTICE_MAIL_SECRET` plus the [shared mail settings](mail.md); `verify_jwt = false` in `config.toml`). **Not built: Discord notices and removing the Discord role**, because no member's Discord account is linked in the app.
  - **Who sees a record**: `_can_see_member` (the member, a parent in `guardian_links`, staff) on every table's select policy; every write is an RPC and direct writes are revoked.
  - `/standing` (avatar menu, students; `?member=` for a linked parent, from the parent dashboard, or staff) and `/standing/team` (staff block: Overview, Absences, Confirm, Log, Incidents, Weeks, Grades, FLL, Settings for admins). Before 0015 both say not set up yet.

- **Absences against the handbook text** (ledger 0016, feedback d56ab62c, `supabase/migrations/0025_absence_handbook.sql` + its test, `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0025.mjs`; decision 48 for what the text leaves open). Compared rule by rule with `src/handbook/handbook.md` Sections 4, 6 and 11. Four mismatches fixed: a medical visit is no longer accepted as an emergency (Section 6 defines one as own illness or injury, or a death, hospital stay or serious illness in close family; the reasons now read "Illness or injury" and "Family emergency (a death, hospital stay or serious illness)"); the emergency deadline is the next SCHOOL day, skipping posted break weeks (`_next_school_day`); a request still unanswered when its meeting started is approved and can no longer be denied afterwards (`decide_absence`); a mentor check-in during a meeting (a manual IN, `staff_add_event`) counts as attending, present inside 15 minutes and late after (`_member_meetings`). Already matching and unchanged: the 24-hour rule, mandatory events (emergency, or a religious holiday 14 days ahead), three lates per absence, 80 percent never rounded, a fully excused week. `_member_meetings` and `_next_school_day` are no longer callable by members (0015 left a definer read of anyone's meeting counts open). 0025 re-defines four 0015 functions: re-pasting 0015 puts the old rules back; paste 0025 after it.

- **The October correction and the hold** (ledger 0034, `supabase/migrations/0044_standing_october_correction.sql` + its test, `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0044.mjs`, `docs/history/standing-october-correction-0034.md`). The first monthly run (2026-11-01) put all 27 students on meetings probation and mailed every family, because nobody had a meeting scan. Now: a session already open at a meeting's start counts present (`_session_open_at`, 12-hour cap); a meeting with no attendance taken at all counts for no one (`_meeting_recorded`); and `standing_evaluate` wraps the 0015 run (`_standing_evaluate_run`, closed to clients) and rolls back any run that would open probation or propose removal for more than `standing_settings.hold_over_pct` percent of judged members (default 25, at least 3), alerting admins once per 20 hours instead (`standing_hold`). The 27 probations were dismissed without notices, October's month rows removed, and `starts_on` set to 2026-11-02. 0044 re-defines 0025's `_member_meetings` and replaces 0015's `standing_evaluate`: apply 0044 again after any re-paste of 0015 or 0025.
