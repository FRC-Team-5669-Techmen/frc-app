# Families and records

- **Families and records** (`supabase/migrations/0017_families_and_records.sql` + its test, `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0017.mjs`, `src/FamilyPage.jsx`, `src/MedicalForm.jsx`, `src/EmergencyInfoPage.jsx`, `src/DriverDocsPage.jsx`, `src/TeamBoardsPage.jsx`, the Extras cards in `src/StandingPage.jsx`, `HandbookChanges` in `src/HandbookPage.jsx`, the task pick and shop-tools line on `/checkin`, `src/dev/fixture/features/families.js`, `tools/e2e/features/families.mjs`; 2026-10-19, ledger 0012). Handbook Sections 1, 9, 10, 13, 16, 17, 18, 19 and 22; needs 0015.
  - **The family link** `/family/<token>`: public, outside `ProtectedLayout`, let through every gate like `/parent/<token>`. The token is the application's existing `parent_token` (supabase/parent_responses.sql), so the two links are the same credential. Every read and write is ONE anon-callable function, `family_call(token, action, args)`: `summary` (the student, open records, recent weeks, the checklist, emergency information, the current handbook version and whether this application's parent signed it), `medical_save`, `groupme`, `sign` (into `parent_acknowledgments`, the current version only). The page renders the handbook text from `src/handbook/handbook.md` on request. Staff email the link, or replace a shared one with a new token (`staff_send_family_link(member, rotate)`, from `/standing?member=`).
  - **Emergency information**: `member_medical` (two contacts required, conditions, allergies, medicines, where carried medicine is). Read by staff and a linked parent only, never the student or other members; written by `save_medical` (a linked parent or staff) or the family link. `/emergency-info` is the staff sheet (`staff_emergency_sheet`, printable) and, with `?member=`, the edit form; the parent dashboard links to it.
  - **Driver documents**: private bucket `driver-docs`, files under `<uploader id>/`; `register_driver_document`, `delete_driver_document` (owner or staff); `driver_documents` readable by the owner and staff. **A staff member opens a file only after `log_driver_document_view` records the view**: the storage read policy (`_driver_doc_readable`) admits staff for two minutes after a logged view, and the owner always. A daily job flags the documents of a driver whose profile is no longer active; `/driver-docs` lists them to staff for deletion.
  - **No-photo list**: `media_exceptions` through `staff_set_media_exception`; read by staff, members on the Media sub-team, and the member themself. `/no-photo`.
  - **Sub-team status**: `subteam_status_reports(week, subteam, green|yellow|red, note)` through `set_subteam_status` (leads and staff); every member reads it. `/status-reports` over `src/subteams.js`.
  - **Hours leaderboard**: `/leaderboard`, client-side over the season's attendance through `sessionsFromEvents` (no migration); FLL hours shown apart. Students only are ranked; anyone holding mentor, admin or parent in `member_roles` is listed apart, unranked, under Mentors and adults (a student lead is a student).
  - **Onboarding checklist** (`_onboarding_json`): member steps (`first_registration`, `discord`, `groupme` in `onboarding_items` via `mark_onboarding`), record steps (SAFE-1 and RESP-1 from the IDEA mirror, emergency information on file, the student handbook signature, a parent signature from the family link, a parent account or a staff-ticked paper one), staff steps (`buddy`, `parent_signed`). Due `standing_settings.checklist_days` (14) after joining. `shop_tools_allowed()` is false until it is done, which `/checkin` shows as WATCH ONLY; `_member_since` now starts the standards at the first full week after joining.
  - **Sub-team training**: `subteam_training(subteam or null, code, required)`, seeded with SAFE-1 and RESP-1 required for all; held means an active, unexpired IDEA mirror row for the member email (`_holds_cert`).
  - **Deadlines**: `onboarding_deadline_check()` daily (13:40 UTC) PROPOSES removal for an overdue checklist and, after `standing_settings.training_due`, for missing required training. An admin confirms on Team standing, as for every removal.
  - **Handbook versions**: `handbook_versions` (seeded with 2026-10-20) through `publish_handbook_version` (admin): effective 7 days later, a safety change at once, every active member notified; the feed and the admin form are on `/handbook`'s Sign tab. The text itself still changes in `src/handbook/` with `HANDBOOK_VERSION`.
  - **Letter progress** (`_letter_json`, the 9 lines of Section 18) and `member_extras(member)` feed the checklist, training and letter cards on `/standing`; staff confirm a sub-team contribution there (`letter_contributions`).
  - **Tasks at sign-in**: after a check-in `/checkin` lists up to 5 open jobs for the member's sub-teams; a tap claims it and sets the session's job (`claim_task`, `set_session_job`, the existing jobs RPCs).
- **Email every family its link**: a button on `/standing/team` (Overview) runs `staff_send_family_link` for every member the standards apply to, after a confirm tap, and reports emails queued and members with no application or parent email. Emails leave through `notice_outbox` and the notice-mail function, so nothing is delivered until that function is deployed.
