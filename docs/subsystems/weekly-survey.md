# Weekly survey

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` (The subsystems) on 2026-10-19, so `CLAUDE.md` stays short. `CLAUDE.md` links here; keep this file current in the same commit as any change to what it describes.

- **Weekly survey** (`supabase/weekly_surveys.sql` + `src/surveys.js` + `src/SurveyPage.jsx`/`.css` + `src/SurveysAdmin.jsx`/`.css`). A short recurring pulse check students fill out on a phone in the shop, SEPARATE from the per-season `member_applications` onboarding form. Four tables: `surveys(title, opens_at, closes_at, is_open, created_by, created_at)`, `survey_questions(survey_id, position, kind[single/multi/scale/text], prompt, options jsonb, required)`, `survey_responses(survey_id, member_id, submitted_at)` unique `(survey_id, member_id)`, `survey_answers(response_id, question_id, value jsonb)` unique `(response_id, question_id)`. **QUESTIONS ARE ROWS, NOT A HARDCODED FORM** — the fifth seeded question is about one October competition and rotates weekly, so nothing about any prompt, option or kind exists in the client. **`surveys_one_open_idx` (`unique on ((true)) where is_open`) makes "the single currently-open survey" a DATABASE guarantee** rather than a client tiebreak; the consequence is that opening B while A is open RAISES, so `SurveysAdmin` closes the open one first as two writes — a trigger would have hidden the rule, and a race between two mentors now ends in an error rather than a second open survey. **`survey_questions_required_chk` enforces `required = false or kind = 'single'`** — only single-choice may be required, written where it cannot drift; the member surface re-states it rather than trusting the row. **RLS**: surveys and questions `select using (true)` to authenticated, write `is_staff()` (the repo's existing mentor check — no new role helper); `survey_responses insert own` is `member_id = auth.uid() AND the survey is_open` (without the `is_open` clause a member could POST into last month's closed dataset); select is own-or-staff on both responses and answers; `survey_answers insert own response` matches through the caller's own response row. **NO UPDATE AND NO DELETE POLICY on responses or answers, for anyone including staff** — a submitted answer is a record of what somebody said at the time, so **editing a submission is NOT OFFERED** (the brief's other option, a server-side resubmit-that-replaces, would need a SECURITY DEFINER RPC and is deliberately not built). `revoke update, delete, truncate ... from authenticated` is required for the same reason feedback.sql needs it — the bootstrap `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES` means policy absence alone fails SILENTLY at 0 rows. **`survey_answers_nonempty_chk` rejects `null` / `""` / `[]`**, which is "a skipped question stores NO ROW" expressed in the schema, so the mentor aggregate never has to filter blanks. **`src/surveys.js` is the shared pure module** (no React, no Supabase): `resolveOpenSurvey` / `sortQuestions` / `isAnswered` / `buildAnswerRows` / `missingRequired` / `isUnconfigured` / `aggregate`. `aggregate`'s percentages are **of ANSWERED, not of everyone who submitted** — every kind but a required single is skippable, so the submission count is the wrong denominator; options render in the mentor's AUTHORED order (never sorted by count) so an option keeps its place week to week, a zero-count option still gets a row, and a value no longer in the option list is appended and marked `unlisted` rather than silently dropped. **`/survey`** is the member surface: one question per card, full-width 48px tap targets, **no `<select>` anywhere**, one scroll and one button; tapping a chosen single option again CLEARS it (an optional single otherwise has no way back to no-answer); a 23505 from the unique constraint resolves to the already-submitted state rather than an error; already-submitted is an explicit card, never a blank form. **A required question whose options are still a TODO is rendered as a visible "no options yet" marker and is NOT enforced as required** — the seeded survey ships in exactly that state, and a form that refuses to submit with no way to fix it from the member's side would be worse. **`/surveys`** is the staff surface: create, author questions with kind chips and reorder, open/close, and results — aggregate by default with **individual attribution behind a deliberate toggle**, because putting names on by default changes what people are willing to type into "what is blocking you". Seeded survey "Week of Aug 31" is open with five questions; **questions 1 and 2 ship with their options as a TODO comment** and must be filled in before it goes out. **`opens_at` on the seed is deliberately NULL** — it was written as 2026-08-31 first and the member surface then correctly reported "no survey open" when pasted on the 30th. Nav: "Weekly Survey" in the avatar menu (non-parent members), "Surveys" in the staff block. **The MANAGEMENT surface landed 2026-08-30** on the `/surveys` Settings tab: a list that carries each survey's state, window, question count and RESPONSE COUNT (eagerly, in the same `loadSurveys` round trip as the list itself -- the response count is what makes a delete safe to press, and a count behind a click is a count nobody looks at), plus rename / re-window, **duplicate**, **CSV export** and **delete**. **Duplicate is the load-bearing one**: the normal week is duplicate last week, swap the rotating question, open -- without it every week is a re-author from an empty form, which is how a weekly survey becomes a monthly one. It copies title (suffixed, counting up), questions, options and required flags, RENUMBERS positions 1..n, copies no responses, and **lands CLOSED, which is exactly what makes it safe while another survey is open**: `surveys_one_open_idx` is partial `where is_open`, so a closed row is not in the index at all. **Delete is two clicks and the second one names what dies by number** ("Delete 'Week of Aug 31'? 3 responses and 7 answers will be deleted."), never a bare are-you-sure; when the armed survey is the LIVE one it says so as well. **The delete relies on ON DELETE CASCADE reaching past the `revoke update, delete, truncate ... from authenticated`, and that was MEASURED against a real PostgreSQL 16 rather than reasoned about** -- a cascade runs as the referencing table's OWNER, so the caller's table grants and RLS are not consulted for the rows it removes; the direct deletes still raise 42501, which is the control that makes the cascade result mean something. The evidence and the "do not fix this by granting delete on the children" warning are recorded at the revokes in the migration. **`surveyState()` is why the pill is not just `is_open`**: is_open true with a passed `closes_at` reads EXPIRED and with a future `opens_at` reads SCHEDULED, because in both cases the member surface shows nothing -- calling those OPEN is the exact confusion the seeded survey caused. **The CSV's Member column is wired to the SAME attribution toggle as the on-screen names**: the column is always present so the sheet's shape does not move, but the values only fill in while the surface is already showing names, because a CSV is the easiest way for an anonymous aggregate to quietly become a named one. One row per response, one column per question, `submitted_at` as the stored ISO instant. **NOT BUILT, deliberately: no edit-after-submit, no reminder push (the `announcements` prefs key exists but has no feature), no per-season scoping, no scale question in the seed** (the kind is supported and authorable, nothing uses it yet)**, no CSV formula-injection guard** (a leading `=` in a student's free text is emitted verbatim, because the export is a record of what was said), **no undo on delete, and no archive-instead-of-delete**.
