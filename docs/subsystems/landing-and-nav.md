# Landing page and nav

The primary navigation remains short (decision 49): Home, Schedule, Scouting, Robot and Hours for members and staff; Home, Schedule and Team Hours for parent-only accounts. Hours contains My hours, Log hours, Team hours and Leaderboard. Scouting leads and drivers are member capabilities or event assignments, not additional application roles. A parent with mentor, lead or admin authority uses the staff view. The brand, fonts, theme tokens and Plate shape language are unchanged.

`src/NavBar.jsx` groups the account menu into My profile, My record, What's new, Preferences and Sign out. Staff additionally see Team admin and its waiting count; a non-staff sign-out approver additionally sees Sign-outs. Every interactive top-level branch counts, including both disclosures: five normally, six for staff or a student approver. This supersedes decision 50's seven-entry default under Mr. Pina's codex-frc-nav instruction. My record holds Standing (non-staff), Handbook, Certifications, My schedule (non-parent), Weekly survey (students), Driver documents and Emergency information (parents). Preferences holds the theme radios and Training / Replay tour. Disclosure and theme interaction keep the menu open. Escape closes and restores trigger focus; outside pointer/focus and navigation dismiss it. The housing stays within the viewport and scrolls while Sign out stays in reach.

Search is always available beside the avatar, including at phone widths. Ctrl+K and Cmd+K toggle `src/CommandPalette.jsx`. Its native modal dialog contains focus, makes the background inert, and restores focus to Search on close. A labeled combobox supports arrows, Home, End, Enter and Escape; results are also tappable. Empty results explain recovery. It searches titles, routes, descriptions and task aliases such as carpool, paperwork and practice. The first member group directly links the onboarding/standing checklist, handbook, certifications, jobs, weekly survey and competition schedule.

`src/navigation.js` derives searchable pages from `src/featureGuide.js`, the same inventory that builds the tour. `tests/feature-guide.test.js` holds that guide to every App route or an explicit exclusion in both directions. Admin pages require admin, not just staff; search and the training provider use the actual admin flag. Parent-only search includes their handbook, certifications, standing and family records. Sign-outs additionally follows the existing approval RPC; an error or missing RPC offers no approval command. Approval state is tied to member identity and reloads on account switches. Search adds no team-data read, write or permission boundary. Page and database authorization remain authoritative.

Parameter pages use the current matching event, trip or match when available. Otherwise each result names the chooser step and opens Scouting, Trips, Event rosters, Meet a team or Match log. The member result opens the viewer's own member page, with other members available from Team Hours. Private family, questionnaire and trip tokens are never enumerated or fabricated; they require their emailed link. Retired route aliases retain their existing redirects; developer pages and login are excluded. The complete per-role inventory and private-link reasons are retained in `tools/e2e/nav-evidence.json`.

The Robot hub (`src/RobotPage.jsx`) retains its nine tools. Team admin (`src/ManagePage.jsx`, catalog `src/adminTools.js`) retains its four groups, waiting-first ordering and tool filter: sixteen tools for mentor/lead and nineteen for admin. `src/HubTile.jsx`, `src/Hub.css` and `src/navIcons.jsx` supply the shared tiles and icons. Home's existing Do this now (`src/doThisNow.js`) remains the contextual route into required student steps. The What's new unread dot and theme persistence continue through `src/updates.js`, `src/themeMode.js` and `src/ThemeSwitch.jsx`.

`tools/e2e/features/navigation.mjs` runs with the standing feature gate at 375, 1152 and 1440, in both themes. It measures the six-entry cap, both directions of role and capability visibility, keyboard/touch search navigation, focus restoration, empty search, contextual Pick board links and missing-migration behavior. `tools/e2e/nav-evidence.mjs` captures member, scout lead, driver, mentor, admin, parent, staff lead, student approver and parent/staff combinations. `tools/e2e/nav-report.mjs` packages paired screenshots and measurements. See `docs/history/codex-frc-nav.md` for baseline counts, red/green evidence and verification limits.

Guided training (codex-frc-tour, 0047) replaces the descriptive tour. The Preferences link opens `/training`; Home invites unfinished learners and Team admin links Training progress. The `src/tour/` engine uses the role-filtered feature catalog, hands-on core activities, device resume and completion records that never treat skip as completion. See [tour.md](tour.md).
