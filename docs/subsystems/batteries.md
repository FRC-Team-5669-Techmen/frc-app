# Battery log

Built by ledger 0017 (migration 0026) for Beach Blitz 2026, after half the team's batteries tested bad and two matches were lost to brownouts.

- **Page `/batteries`** (`src/BatteriesPage.jsx`, `src/BatteriesPage.css`). A board of battery cards, worst first: big label, status pill, Check and Ready pills, last resting voltage, brownout count, run count, last used, and four one-tap charge buttons (charged, charging, needs charge, in robot). Filters: In service, Match-ready, Check, Retired. Tapping a card opens its history; Log a run opens a phone-first form (battery and kind remembered in `localStorage`, decimal keypads, one Save). CSV of every run. Staff see Add batteries (a numbered set, B01 to B12) and Edit battery (label, brand, year, status including retired, notes). Parent-only accounts are told it is for students and staff.
- **Rules in one file, `src/batteries.js`**, read by the page AND the connector's `list_batteries`. `CHECK_THRESHOLDS` holds the proposed defaults (a brownout in the last 3 runs, a resting voltage before a match under 12.5 V, resistance over 20 milliohms). Match-ready is competition, charged and not flagged. A measurement left blank is NULL, never 0, everywhere: form, database, CSV.
- **Database (0026).** `batteries`, `battery_runs`; select for `_battery_member_ok()` (approved, active, a student or staff role, not parent-only); no client write grant. Every write is a SECURITY DEFINER function through `writeVia` with no direct fallback: `battery_log_run` and `battery_set_charge` (students and staff), `battery_edit_run` (the author for 24 hours, staff always), `battery_save` and `battery_add_set` (staff). A match, practice match or drive practice marks a charged or in-robot battery as needs charge (a bench test does not). Nothing is deleted; a dead battery is retired.
- **Connector.** `list_batteries` (read), `log_battery_run` (confirm), `set_battery_charge` (undo: the reverse sets the previous state the function answers). Labels resolve through `resolveBattery` in `api/_lib/connector/resolve.js` ("b7", "B07", or "7" when only one battery is numbered 7).
- **Proof.** `0026_battery_log_rls_test.sql` (20 checks) and `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0026.mjs` (9 mutants); `tests/batteries.test.js`, `tests/connector-batteries.test.js`; `tools/e2e/features/batteries.mjs` on fixture mode (`src/dev/fixture/features/batteries.js`); the connector proof's battery samples.
