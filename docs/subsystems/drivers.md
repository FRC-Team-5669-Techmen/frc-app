# Driver leaderboard

Built by ledger 0020 (migration 0029) so choosing a driver rests on a record: who has driven the real robot and for how long, who has practiced in MoSim (the FRC driving simulator), and what the coaches saw. The record is kept across seasons; the page scores one season at a time.

- **Page `/drivers`** (`src/DriversPage.jsx`, `src/Drivers.css` on top of `src/BatteriesPage.css`; tokens only, so a light theme that redefines the `src/theme.css` tokens restyles it). Reached from the Drivers tile on `/robot`. A season picker (the current season by default, every season, and All time), a tally (drivers ranked, robot time, MoSim time, your rank), the leaderboard sorted by Driver Score (rank, name, score, robot hours, MoSim hours, coach average or Unrated; your row is marked), a "How the Driver Score works" disclosure that prints the formula from `SCORE_RULES`, a Log a session form (kind: practice field, match, test, MoSim; minutes; date; MoSim score; notes) and My drive log. Tapping a row opens the driver's sheet: score breakdown, coach ratings (criteria; the note only where the reader may see it), sessions. Staff also get a Driver picker on the log form, Rate a driver (five criteria 1 to 5, each optional, and a note), Edit on any session and Void on any session. A student can void their own session for 24 hours after logging it. Parent-only accounts are told it is for students and staff. Before 0029 is pasted the page says Not set up yet.
- **The score lives in ONE file, `src/driverScore.js`**, read by the page, the `/robot` tile and the connector's `list_drivers`. The SQL stores facts and never scores. Practice hours = robot hours + `SIM_WEIGHT` (0.35) x MoSim hours; experience = 1 - e^(-hours / `HOURS_SCALE` 10); coach = the mean of each coach's newest rating in the season (each rating the mean of the criteria given), 1..5 mapped to 0..1, unrated 0; Driver Score = round(100 x (0.5 x experience + 0.5 x coach)). Voided sessions count for nothing. A MoSim score is shown but not scored. Sessions are in a season by `session_date`, ratings by their LA date. These weights are proposed defaults.
- **Database (0029).** `drive_sessions` (member, date, minutes 1 to 600, kind, MoSim score only on a MoSim session, notes, logged_by, void columns) and `driver_ratings` (driver, coach, five nullable criteria with at least one given, note, rated_at; never self). Select on sessions for `_driver_member_ok()` (approved, active, student or staff, not parent-only); select on ratings for staff and the driver it is about; `driver_rating_scores()` hands every eligible member the ratings WITHOUT notes, which is what the leaderboard reads. No client write grant. Writes are SECURITY DEFINER functions through `writeVia` with no direct fallback: `driver_log_session` (own; staff for anyone; a student within 60 days), `driver_edit_session` (staff), `driver_void_drive` (staff; the driver for 24 hours), `driver_rate` (staff, not themselves, only an approved active student or staff member). Nothing is deleted. 0029 replaces `_merge_member_rules` with the six driver columns: paste it again after any re-paste of 0020, 0022, 0024, 0026 or 0028.
- **Connector.** `list_drivers` (read, `season: current | all`); the four writes are generated `call_app` entries that ask first.
