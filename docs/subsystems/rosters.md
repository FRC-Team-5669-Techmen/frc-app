# Event rosters

- **Event rosters** (`supabase/migrations/0016_event_rosters.sql` + its test, `tools/sql-harness/mutants-0016.mjs`, `src/RostersPage.jsx`, `src/dev/fixture/features/eventrosters.js`, `tools/e2e/features/rosters.mjs`; 2026-10-19, ledger 0011). Handbook Sections 4, 13, 16 and 18; needs 0015.
  - **Roles**: `event_roles(event, member, role, reason)` through `staff_assign_role` (staff, a reason always, "record why") and `staff_unassign_role`. Roles: driver, operator, human player, drive coach, pit crew (the field group), Impact presenter and pit speaker (speaking), scouting lead, scout, media, safety captain.
  - **Eligibility** is ONE function, `event_role_blocker(member, event, role)`, which the assign RPC enforces and the page asks first: a suspension over the event days blocks everything; meetings or hours probation (open or proposed removal) blocks the field and speaking groups; grades probation blocks the field group; missing Kickoff (`standing_settings.kickoff_event_id`, set on Team standing Settings) without a check-in, a present mark or an approved emergency absence blocks the field group, for a member who had joined by then. `event_eligibility_check()` runs daily (pg_cron 13:20 UTC) and tells the admins about any role a member is no longer eligible for in the next day; it never removes one.
  - **Posting**: `staff_post_roster` writes `event_rosters` and notifies everyone with a role; it returns the days ahead and the page says when that is under the handbook's 10. A draft roster is readable by staff only; a posted one by every member.
  - **Event-day attendance**: `event_attendance(event, day, member, status)` through `staff_mark_event_day`; absent from a competition or mandatory event day without an approved absence opens meetings probation (`_open_probation`).
  - **Conflicts**: `member_conflicts` through `declare_conflict` / `withdraw_conflict` (an upcoming event only); `conflict_deadline` is February 1 for an event January to June. A declared conflict excuses nothing.
  - **Family sign-up** (only when the event family hub, 0005 to 0010, is applied; the block is skipped otherwise and the file must be pasted again after): `hub_events.event_id` links a hub to a calendar event (the roster page's Family sign-up card), `hub_events.mandatory`; `staff_remind_signups(hub)` reminds every roster family without a finished sign-up (`_hub_progress` phase1_done) and records `event_signup_reminders`; `event_signup_check()` (hourly) opens meetings probation for a family still missing 48 hours after the reminder.
  - **Carpool lock**: the trigger `carpool_lock_guard` on `hub_seats` (insert, update, delete) and `hub_cars` (insert, delete): inside 72 hours before the day's `meet_at` (or `venue_opens_at`) only staff change it, inside 12 hours nobody does; a cascade (a family or event removed) is not blocked. Marking a car Left or Arrived (an update) is never blocked.
  - `/rosters` (avatar menu, non-parents) lists competitions and mandatory events; `/rosters/:id` shows roles to everyone once posted, and to staff the assign form, Post, Attendance, Conflicts and Family sign-up.
  - Meals and allergies without names are the hub's existing food sign-up.
