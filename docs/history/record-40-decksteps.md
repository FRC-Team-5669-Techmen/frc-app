 Earlier - 2026-08-24 (DeckSteps — stepped reveal, the capability the last three deck specs asked for and could not have. A sibling BEHAVIOUR component to DeckStage, not a prop on twenty-six patterns and not part of DeckStage: it is mounted once, intercepts next/prev, and writes data-step onto the active sheet. DECISIONS, AND WHY: opt-in per sheet rather than automatic, because mounting it must never re-pace a deck already written and the only automatic escape would be the per-pattern opt-out prop this exists to avoid; re-entry by DIRECTION rather than memory (forward -> 1, backward -> last, any other arrival -> last), because a jump is navigation and a half-revealed sheet is the wrong answer to a question from the floor. THE COUNT IS IN JS BECAUSE CSS CANNOT DO IT — :nth-child() reads parentage and the runtime interposes hosts, so a ladder would count wrappers. ONE AUDIT NARROWING, PAID FOR: check 22 forbids any rule-level opacity:0/visibility:hidden and its stated reason is that such a rule "hides it in print, in PDF and under reduced motion too" — so the exemption is shaped to answer exactly that (gate + step-item subject only), hiding under reduced motion is the REQUIREMENT, and new check 30 refuses to pass without the @media print release. Control 30f moves the same declaration out of the gate and must still fail. VERIFIED IN A BROWSER AT /_ds, LOOKED AT AND NOT ONLY MEASURED, because a reveal is motion: nine compact RoleCards stepped 1 -> 5 -> 9 with cards 1-5 in the IDENTICAL grid cells at step 5 and step 9 (space is reserved, so nothing reflows as you step), and the no-DeckSteps deck rendering pixel-equivalent to step 9. Eleven verdicts hold: count 9, 9 real host nodes between group and items (three flavours: x-host inline-transparent, dc-host transparent from a stylesheet, span[data-frc-host]), contiguous order at every stage, interception (one key advances the step and NOT the sheet), forward boundary -> next sheet at step 1, backward boundary -> previous sheet at its LAST step (9/9), a no-group sheet with no data-step and no marks, pending visibility:hidden + opacity:0 against revealed visible, and a base-state deck at 9 cards / 0 hidden / no data-step / 0 marks. Escapes measured directly: @media print at step 1 shows 9 of 9; prefers-reduced-motion shows 1 of 9 at step 1 and 4 of 9 at step 4 with transition-duration 0s. TWO REAL FINDINGS CAME OUT OF LOOKING, both recorded at the section: (1) the first version wrapped the nine cards as SampleGrid children and rendered ZERO host nodes — pickSlots does its job and splices a host out of the React tree before it reaches the DOM — so the host claim was false and the hosts now sit in plain markup where they survive; (2) entry state is applied from a MutationObserver callback, which is a MICROTASK, so a synchronous read after a key press sees no data-step and reports the forward boundary broken when it is the measurement that is early. The proof now reads inside requestAnimationFrame, and a dedicated beforePaint check pins the guarantee that actually matters: at the forward boundary data-step is already "1" before the browser paints, so no frame is ever painted ungated. ds:audit ok at 79 components / 293 files, 46+9 = 55 controls, production build clean, DS still absent from dist. NOT DONE, deliberately: no sheet pattern changed, no public API changed, CoverSheet and ClosingSheet untouched.
