 Earlier the same day - Anomaly resolve block gained a half-hour stepper and a one-click void, both scoped to `EventRow`'s `autoFocusEmpty` branch in src/MemberHoursAdmin.jsx. See the (a)/(b) sentences in the accountability bullet. THE POINT IS THAT NEITHER WEAKENS THE NO-PRE-FILL RULE: the stepper's offset starts at 0 and `outTime` still mounts at `''`, so every value in that field is traceable to a click or a keystroke - the stepper removes the ARITHMETIC, not the judgement. The one design decision worth keeping: `outTime` is the single source of truth rather than a separate offset counter, which is what makes "whichever was touched most recently wins" true by construction instead of by reconciliation - typing simply becomes the baseline the next click reads. The low clamp CLEARS the field rather than pinning at the check-in, so stepping down is a real undo back to nothing-entered and a zero-length session is unreachable. The void button's two-click shape was chosen so the existing required-reason field IS the confirmation, per the brief's "reuse that pattern"; the side effect is that a misclick on a red button next to "Add check-out" costs nothing.
