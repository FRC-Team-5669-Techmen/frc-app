 Earlier the same day — Three scoped design-system changes, nothing else touched. (1) DeckFooter gained a third `mark` value, `"mark"` — `MarkGlyph variant="auto"`, the helmet alone, no lettering — with `"logotype"` kept as the default so no existing deck changes, and the rail mark height raised 32 → 56px for both. The height is the substantive part: 32px is 2.2% of the 1440 frame, which is the usual 1/50 projection floor, and that floor is for plain type rather than for artwork with hairline strokes and tight counters. 56px is 3.9%, still leaves 16px of clearance inside the 96px rail, puts the logotype at 205px wide against the 200px FIRST zone next to it, and stays under that zone's 60px height so a team mark never out-sizes the FIRST lockup on an external deck; `mark="seal"` keeps its own 64px because a seal is square.
