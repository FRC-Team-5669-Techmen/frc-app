 Earlier the same day — App gold corrected to the published Techmen Gold, against FRC_Design_System.md v1.4. src/theme.css --gold #D4AF37 -> #FFE629, with --gold-bright (#FFF06B), --gold-dim (#C7B320), --gold-a08/a14/a24 and --hr-regular/--hr-build all re-derived from it; two hardcoded rgba(212,175,55,.08) washes in MemberSkillsHome.css and ParentHomePage.css were repointed at var(--gold-a08) so the token is again the only source. src/lib/design-system/ was NOT touched: its tokens are already canonical and scoped under .frc-deck. AREA, NOT SATURATION: #FFE629 is brighter, so instead of dulling it the large gold FIELDS were reduced. Six surfaces changed from a gold slab to a gold label + gold border on the dark plate, with the fill kept for :hover/:focus-visible (the active state gold is actually for): .landing-cta, .gate-submit, .gate-signout, .login-card button[type=submit], .pr-submit, .ma-submit. The threshold was measured, not guessed: those are the only solid-gold controls that are a full-width or hero-scale field (>=6000px^2 rendered, or width:100%/flex:1 in CSS). Everything else that fills with gold is a small control - dots, chips, view tabs, progress and trend bars, per-row action buttons - where small-area gold is exactly the active state the brand asks for, so ~60 such rules were deliberately left alone. VERIFIED IN A BROWSER with before/after captures of the landing page, the access gate and the splash (artifacts/gold-before, gold-after, plus an intermediate gold-after-token set showing the token change alone): after the change there are ZERO gold background fills on the landing page and the access gate, and every gold declaration left is a label, a border or a rule; the mark, the eyebrow, the headings and the button labels all measure rgb(255,230,41). The splash is PIXEL-IDENTICAL before and after, which is correct: it carries only the mark on --bg and no gold chrome. The login card was rendered too, since its submit was one of the six. scripts/gen-icons.js REWRITTEN rather than deleted: it wrote solid #005bff squares over three correct mark-derived icons. It now rasterises public/assets/logos/Mark-Gold.svg through headless Chrome and REFUSES TO RUN unless that file hashes to the value in PROVENANCE.json, so a recolored mark can never reach every home screen at once. Its scales were set by measuring the ink bounding box of the shipped icons and matching them (51.6-52.2% vs a shipped 52.0-52.2%; maskable 38.5% vs 38.7%), and --check / --out let it be verified before it writes. The shipped icons were left in place: they are correct and hand-tuned, and regenerating them would be churn. Both `hazards` entries in src/lib/design-system/assets/PROVENANCE.json (the app --gold, and gen-icons) were then updated to status `resolved 2026-08-23` with found/done/note fields, kept rather than deleted so the reason the hash guard exists stays readable.
